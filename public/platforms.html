2. Detailed Platform Breakdown

YouTube

Reeva supports standard public YouTube videos, YouTube Shorts, and educational lectures. Format combinations are evaluated server-side to deliver synchronized video and audio streams. Playlists, live streams, and paid rental movies are not supported.

Twitter / X

Reeva queries official media syndication and CDN delivery endpoints to locate the highest available bitrate MP4 variant for public status tweets containing video attachments.

Pinterest

Reeva extracts direct video file streams from public Pinterest video pins across desktop and mobile sharing URLs.

Meta Platforms (Instagram & Facebook)

Reeva queries public metadata for Reels and public video posts. In production environments where access is permitted and unauthenticated media URLs are returned, direct downloads are initiated. When platform policies enforce authentication or access restrictions, Reeva automatically activates the official sandboxed embedded player.

TikTok

For public short-form videos outside geographically restricted regions, direct video files are streamed. In jurisdictions subject to national regulatory blocks or anti-scraping challenge pages, Reeva seamlessly presents the official sandboxed player.

3. Strictly Prohibited & Unsupported Content

  • Private accounts, secret boards, and followers-only posts.
  • DRM-encrypted streams (Widevine, FairPlay, PlayReady).
  • Paywalled streaming platforms (Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Amazon Prime).
  • Copyrighted commercial music tracks separated from their original context.
