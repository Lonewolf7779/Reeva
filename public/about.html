About Reeva

Open-source, hardened technical utility for public media preservation.

1. Mission & Vision

The modern web is transient. Content published to social media networks and video sharing platforms frequently disappears due to policy shifts, algorithmic delisting, account removals, or platform sunsetting. Reeva was developed as an open-source engineering response to this fragility: a fast, secure, minimalist media utility that allows creators, researchers, journalists, and everyday users to retrieve and preserve publicly accessible media for personal, educational, and archival use.

2. Security by Design

Unlike ad-cluttered downloader websites that expose users to invasive popups, redirect loops, and malicious third-party scripts, Reeva adheres to uncompromising security principles:

  • Zero Popups or Malicious Advertising: Reeva contains zero aggressive ad scripts, fake download buttons, or intrusive tracking software.
  • Strict TLS Certificate Verification: Reeva rejects insecure connections, man-in-the-middle proxies, and invalid leaf signatures, guaranteeing that media extraction communicates strictly with official endpoints.
  • DNS-Pinned Anti-SSRF Defense: Sockets are validated prior to connection to forbid interaction with internal networks, loopback addresses, or cloud metadata services.
  • Isolated Runner Sandboxing: External helper binaries operate in restricted subprocesses with explicit timeouts and process isolation.

3. Ephemeral Scratch Storage

Reeva operates under a strict data-minimization model. We do not maintain a permanent video hosting service, media catalog, or user file repository. When format transcoding or video/audio track combination is required, temporary artifacts are written to a restricted local scratch space. An automated cleanup daemon unlinks and purges all expired artifacts every 60 seconds with an absolute 10-minute maximum lifespan.

4. Honest Architectural Transparency

Reeva values technical honesty above all else. We do not claim universal compatibility or promise downloads for media restricted by access controls:

  • We do not bypass CAPTCHAs, paywalls, or authentication boundaries.
  • We do not circumvent digital rights management (DRM) or geographic bans.
  • When upstream platform terms forbid direct binary extraction, Reeva gracefully falls back to official sandboxed embedded player integration rather than failing silently or fabricating downloads.
