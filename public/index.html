Architecture & Security

Strict TLS Verification
Zero man-in-the-middle bypasses. Validates official platform certificates end-to-end.
Anti-SSRF Protections
DNS-pinned socket requests strictly prohibiting access to internal or private IP ranges.
Ephemeral Scratch Storage
No media persistence. Files are automatically deleted after 10 minutes by a background daemon.
Sandboxed Fallback Player
