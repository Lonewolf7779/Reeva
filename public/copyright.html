Legal & Intellectual Property

Copyright & Takedown Policy

Reeva respects intellectual property rights and adheres strictly to digital copyright frameworks, including the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and equivalent international regulations.

1. Technical Role as a Transient Utility

Reeva is an ephemeral client-side media resolution and analysis utility. Reeva is not a content hosting provider, video portal, search engine, or media repository. Reeva operates as follows:

  • Reeva does not publish, curate, index, catalog, or host video or audio files.
  • Reeva does not maintain any permanent library of social media content.
  • When a user requests media resolution, processing occurs in transient runtime memory or designated scratch storage for immediate client delivery. Scratch files are strictly locked during streaming and automatically unlinked upon stream completion or expiration.
  • Because Reeva does not store persistent media files, removing content from the source platform (such as YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, or Pinterest) immediately and permanently prevents any further extraction through Reeva.

2. Respect for Rights Holders & Personal Archiving

Reeva is engineered to facilitate legitimate personal archiving, educational analysis, creator backup of self-published material, and fair use research. Reeva does not promote or facilitate copyright infringement, piracy, or commercial redistribution of copyrighted works without proper rights or statutory exceptions.

3. Notice and Takedown Procedure (DMCA Compliance)

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized agent acting on behalf of a rights holder, and you believe that your protected work is accessible through Reeva in violation of your rights, you may submit a formal notification to our designated contact.

To ensure rapid processing, your notice must include all of the following statutory requirements:

  1. Physical or Electronic Signature: A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  2. Identification of the Protected Work: Clear identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or a representative list if multiple works are covered.
  3. Identification of the Source Material: Identification of the specific public URL(s) on the upstream third-party platform that you request to have blocked from extraction through Reeva.
  4. Contact Information: Sufficient information to enable the operator to contact you, including legal name, postal address, telephone number, and email address.
  5. Good Faith Statement: A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. Accuracy & Penalty of Perjury Statement: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that the complaining party is authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

4. Designated Contact & Response Timeframe

Copyright notices should be sent to the deployment operator contact. As Reeva is an open architecture project deployed by independent infrastructure operators, notices concerning a specific live installation must be directed to that installation's administrative contact:

Deployment Operator Contact: In accordance with deployment configuration, administrative notices may be submitted via the contact channel specified in our Contact Us page or to the operator address listed in the Privacy Policy.

Expected Turnaround: Valid notices containing all required statutory elements are acknowledged and processed within two (2) business days. Remedial actions may include domain-level or URL pattern blocking within the server validation layer.

5. Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe that access to a URL was disabled or blocked as a result of a mistake or misidentification, you may submit a written counter-notification. The counter-notification must contain:

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the material that was removed or to which access was disabled.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  • Your legal name, address, telephone number, and consent to local jurisdiction.

6. Repeat Infringer Policy

Reeva adheres to a strict repeat infringer policy. While Reeva does not maintain user accounts or persistent profiles, our technical controls include IP-based rate limiting, CIDR block enforcement, and domain-level exclusion filters. Systems or networks determined to be engaging in repeated systematic infringement or automated scraping will have access permanently denied.
